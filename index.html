01 · About

From mechanism to design

I am a fourth-year Applied Chemistry student at Xi'an Jiaotong-Liverpool University in Suzhou. My research with Dr Xiaotai Wang spans two Summer Undergraduate Research Fellowship (SURF) projects on nickel-mediated coupling and cyclisation, plus related cobalt-system calculations.

I am interested in how coordination environments and ligand structure shape reactivity and selectivity. I want to move from interpreting catalytic mechanisms towards computational catalyst design and property prediction, with longer-term interest in functional materials such as semiconductors, high-performance polymers and metal-alloy catalysts.

Outside chemistry I enjoy fiction and games, including Wuthering Waves, Minecraft and Slay the Spire.

03 · Software

Tools for auditable energetics

Scientific software · Python + Qt

GaussianReadPro

A local desktop workbench for reading Gaussian output files and building reaction energy profiles whose every number can be traced back to its source job.

  • Import and inspect: batch-read log files and trace energies to source jobs.
  • Compare pathways: relative-energy expressions, explicit references, interactive 2D and 3D profiles.
  • Save and share: restore projects; export images, standalone HTML, tables and source audits.
  • Work locally: Chinese and English interface, charts bundled for offline use.
